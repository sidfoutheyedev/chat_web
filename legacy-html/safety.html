📋 Community Rules

These rules apply to all users. Violations result in immediate ban.

✅
DO: Treat Others Respectfully

Be kind and respectful. Healthy conversation is encouraged.

🚫
NEVER: Share Illegal Content

Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is reported to law enforcement immediately.

🚫
NEVER: Harass or Bully

Harassment, hate speech, or targeted abuse will result in a permanent ban.

🚫
NEVER: Share Personal Information

Don't share your real name, address, phone number, or financial information.

🚫
NEVER: Use if Under 18

This platform is for adults only. Underage users will be banned immediately.

⚠️ How to Report a User

If someone is making you uncomfortable or violating our rules, here's what to do:

1️⃣
Click Report

Click the ⚠️ Report button in any chat window

2️⃣
Select Reason

Choose the violation type from the list

3️⃣
Block & Move On

Block the user and find a new stranger instantly

For serious incidents (threats, CSAM, etc.), email us directly at safety@chatsphere.com with details. We respond within 24 hours.

💡 Safety Tips

  • 🔒
    Never share personal info

    Don't give out your real name, address, school, workplace, or social media handles to strangers.

  • 🚫
    Block immediately if uncomfortable

    You don't owe anyone your time. Block and skip freely — that's what it's there for.

  • 📸
    Be careful with your camera

    Think before showing your face, home, or location on video chat.

  • 💰
    Never send money

    Anyone asking for money, gift cards, or financial help is likely a scammer.
