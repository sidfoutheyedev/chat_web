Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: January 1, 2024 🌐 Effective: January 1, 2024

Summary: ChatSphere is designed to be anonymous. We collect minimal data, we don't store your chats, and we never sell your information. This policy explains what little data we do use and why.

1. Who We Are

ChatSphere ("we", "us", "our") operates the website chatsphere.com (the "Service"). We provide an anonymous chat platform that connects users with strangers worldwide.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: privacy@chatsphere.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to operate the Service.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Nickname: Optional. You may choose a nickname to display. We don't require real names.
  • Interest Tags: Optional topics you select to match with similar users.
  • Reports: If you report a user, we collect the reason and time of the report.

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • IP Address: Collected temporarily to prevent abuse, spam, and illegal activity. Not stored long-term.
  • Browser Type & Version: Used for technical compatibility.
  • Device Type: Mobile or desktop, for responsive design.
  • Cookies: Session cookies for age verification and preferences. See Section 6.
  • Log Data: Server access logs including timestamp, page visited, and browser string. Retained for 30 days.

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store chat messages or conversation content.
  • We do not require email, phone number, or real name.
  • We do not collect payment information (the Service is free).
  • We do not track your location beyond country-level (via IP).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Service: Matching you with strangers, managing chat sessions.
  • Safety & Moderation: Detecting and removing abusive, illegal, or violating content.
  • Technical Operations: Ensuring the platform runs correctly, debugging issues.
  • Analytics: Understanding how the Service is used (via aggregated, anonymized data).
  • Legal Compliance: Responding to lawful requests from authorities when required by law.

4. Advertising

ChatSphere uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and other technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to ChatSphere and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

4.1 Third-Party Ad Partners

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website. These third-party vendors' use of cookies is subject to their own privacy policies, not ours.

4.2 Opting Out

  • Visit aboutads.info to opt out of interest-based advertising.
  • Visit networkadvertising.org for more options.
  • Use your browser's ad blocker or private browsing mode.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Hosting, CDN, and infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by valid legal process (court orders, law enforcement requests).
  • Safety: To prevent fraud, abuse, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights or safety.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use the following cookies and browser storage:

  • cs_age_verified (localStorage): Stores whether you have confirmed you are 18+. Prevents the age gate from showing every visit.
  • safe_banner_dismissed (sessionStorage): Tracks if you've dismissed the safe site banner this session.
  • cs_notif_shown (localStorage): Tracks if the notification permission prompt has been shown.
  • Google AdSense Cookies: Set by Google for ad serving and measurement. See Google's cookie policy for details.

You can clear these at any time via your browser settings. The Service will continue to work without them (except age verification will re-appear).

7. Data Retention

  • Chat Messages: Not stored. Deleted immediately when the session ends.
  • Server Logs: Retained for 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • IP Addresses: Retained for 7 days for abuse prevention, then deleted.
  • Abuse Reports: Retained for 90 days for moderation purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (note: since we collect very little, there may be nothing to delete).
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Objection: Object to processing for certain purposes.
  • GDPR (EU/EEA users): You have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation.
  • CCPA (California users): You have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@chatsphere.com.

9. Children's Privacy

ChatSphere is strictly for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover that a person under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it. If you believe a minor is using this Service, please contact us immediately at safety@chatsphere.com.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • WebSocket Secure (WSS) for chat connections.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Restricted access to any stored data.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

Response time: Within 30 days of receiving your request.
